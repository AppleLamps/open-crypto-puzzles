# Open leads: Objective Thune, "Le secret de la Licorne"

Full notes. The README shows the ranked summary.

## 1. Write to the authors or the publisher

The publisher, PVH editions (Neuchatel, Switzerland), is still active in 2026 and
publicly funded by the Swiss federal culture office for 2026-2028; it lists a public
contact address, `contact@pvh-editions.com`. Jacques Favier, one of the 2 authors,
publishes his own contact email on his blog. The publisher has stated in writing that it
does not know which 3 of the 210 copies hold the keys, so this cannot recover the
numbers directly, but it can answer one factual question with real value: has the lot
ever been claimed? Cost: zero, a single email. Confirmed or killed by a reply, one way or
the other.

## 2. The 2025 CC BY-SA print files

The publisher announced on 2025-05-02 that the book was released under CC BY-SA,
including a link to the full print files without crop marks
(`ludom.cc/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Objective_Thune_print_files_without_crop_marks.zip`).
That link returns HTTP 404 today and is not captured by the Wayback Machine or
archive.today. If it reappears, it gives free, complete access to the book's interior.
It would not by itself reveal which physical copies are marked, since the 3 keys were
inserted physically and at random, but it removes the marketplace/library route as the
only way to see the full text. Cost: periodic recheck, no compute.

## 3. Locate a physical numbered collector copy

About 128 of the 210 collector copies had sold by 2021-01-16 (data/print_run_stock.csv).
Buyers chose their own copy number at purchase, and copy 21 was pre-reserved; whether the
3 "unicorn" numbers were withheld from public selection the same way is an open,
unverified question. No registry of who bought which number has surfaced. Cost: needs a
person, potentially a purchase; confirmed by locating a copy and inspecting it physically.

negative result. Cost: minutes, no compute, pending a clear read window.
