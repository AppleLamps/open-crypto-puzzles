# Author posts: Objective Thune, "Le secret de la Licorne"

Short verbatim quotes from the publisher and authors, with dates and links. No book
text, no page scans: see the copyright note in the README.

---

**2020-03-01**, [bitcoin.fr, "Le secret de la Licorne"](https://bitcoin.fr/le-secret-de-la-licorne/):

> "Trois ouvrages parmi deux-cent-dix collectors contiennent des cles privees qui, une
> fois reunies, donnent acces a un tresor de 21 millions de satoshis actuellement enfoui
> a cette adresse : `3Jf995GANG4EmFBK89byNNyZdtB3ELXJsZ`."
>
> ["Three books among two hundred and ten collector copies contain private keys that,
> once reunited, give access to a treasure of 21 million satoshis currently buried at
> this address."]

**Undated, collector-edition product page** (archived by the Wayback Machine,
capture 20210116213114 of `plaisirvaleurdhistoire.com`):

> "Les exemplaires de collection possedent un dos toile et une couverture legerement
> differente. Ils sont numerotes de 1 a 210 et parmi eux il y a trois licornes qui
> cachent un secret."
>
> ["The collector copies have a cloth spine and a slightly different cover. They are
> numbered 1 to 210 and among them are three unicorns that hide a secret."]

**2020-02-29**, [journalducoin.com](https://journalducoin.com), co-written with the
publisher:

> "Il inséra au hasard les clés privées au coeur de trois ouvrages de collection qui
> furent soigneusement mélangés afin que même lui ignore quels sont les trois Licornes.
> Puis il détruisit par le feu les dernières traces de ces clés."
>
> ["He inserted the private keys at random into three collector copies, which were then
> carefully shuffled so that even he does not know which three are the Unicorns. He then
> destroyed the last traces of these keys by fire."]

**Header illustration of the 2020-03-01 bitcoin.fr article**: a pastiche of the "Secret
of the Unicorn" parchment, reading in part "Trois pairs unys ... codant sous Bitcoin Core
0.18.1 ... le # de l'Hydre" ("three pairs united ... coding under Bitcoin Core 0.18.1
... the hash of the Hydra"), confirming a multisig built with Bitcoin Core 0.18.1
semantics. I have not reproduced this image here; see the README's copyright note.

Source and further detail: [bitcoin.fr/le-secret-de-la-licorne/](https://bitcoin.fr/le-secret-de-la-licorne/), 2020-03-01.
