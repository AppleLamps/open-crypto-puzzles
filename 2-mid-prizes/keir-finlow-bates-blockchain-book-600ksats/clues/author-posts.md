# Author posts and quotes

Short, dated material published by Keir Finlow-Bates about this puzzle series. Quotes are
kept to a sentence or two each; the puzzles themselves live inside the two books, which
are commercial products I do not reproduce here (see the README for page and figure
references and purchase links).

## 2025-09-11, Medium, "Everyone Loves a Treasure Hunt"

https://kf106.medium.com/everyone-loves-a-treasure-hunt-93885ae8d80a

The author's only public write-up about the hunt. It names all 12 addresses and their
status (solved or open at the time of writing) but no clue text. Two quotes from the
article:

> "These won't help you, because the private keys are in the book."

> "I do not have a record of the private keys for any of the twelve puzzles out there, nor
> do I have notes explaining how the puzzles are constructed."

## Front matter, "Move Over Brokers, Here Comes The Blockchain" (print-only)

Present in the physical print runs of the book, absent from the ebook captures I hold:

> "You hold the source of each and every key in your hands... as long as you have a
> physical copy, that is."

## Front matter, "Scansatevi Broker" (Italian edition), Ringraziamenti all'edizione italiana

The passage that carries the IT_hard answer (see README, Solution). It thanks three named
people for a trip through four cities, and closes:

> "Non sarei riuscito a farlo senza voi tre." (I could not have done it without you
> three.)
