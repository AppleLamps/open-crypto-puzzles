# Author material, Smith, Lyle & Moore "Glimmer" treasure hunt

Verbatim quotes from the puzzle site, [smithlylemoore.com/treasure-hunt](https://www.smithlylemoore.com/treasure-hunt),
credited on the page to Thom Miles Royle, copyright 2021. The hunt itself launched with the
escrow funding on 2022-07-29. I reached every quote below through the site's own published
entry chain (its content is served only after the correct password for that page, all of
which I hold except for the 3 quoted at the end).

## Entry page

Opening phrase, entry point of the hunt:

> I Be The Ruler of the Seven Seas

The entry image carries EXIF GPS coordinates that double as the passwords for the next two
pages; the site frames the hunt as: some paths lead nowhere, others lead to the treasure.

## The three unopened gates

Each of the following is the full riddle text of a password-gated page whose password I have
not found. I hold the passwords for every page that leads up to each one.

West branch, page reached after solving an earlier pirate-themed riddle:

> Pirates... Are they coming from the treasure? Did they steal the treasure for themselves?
> Did they download a lot of music/movies from napster/limewire/kazaa/somewhere else? Is that
> Blackbeard? Or Shawn Fanning? Do you feel unbridaled rage, and a newfound desire to play the
> drums? Is your last name Ulrich? Ulbricht? Svartholm? Neij?

East branch, page reached after a deep-sea/narcosis riddle:

> 20,000 Leagues. Impressive. But it's not easy on the body. Hydrogen narcosis sets in. Images
> start flashing before your eyes. Is it your life flashing before your eyes or hallucinations?
> Is this the end?

South branch, page titled "Name 6", reached after naming the other five Gilligan's Island
castaways in sequence:

> The other guy had some good ideas about how to get out of the mess we were in.

This page's own address slug is itself an author-written pun on the show it is quoting:
`havingfunwiththeurl-ilovedthisshowasakid`.
